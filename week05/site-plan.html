Site Plan: The Daily Palette

Created for W05 Project: Site Plan and Build

Site Name

Name: The Daily Palette

Reasoning: I chose this name because "Palette" makes me think of an artist’s workspace, and "Daily" implies that users should come back every day to practice. It’s a simple way to say the site gives you new colors and new ideas every time you visit.

Site Purpose

The goal of this site is to help digital artists who don't know what to draw. It will show a cool piece of art from a museum to give you ideas, and it has a button you can click to get a random drawing prompt. If you see an idea you really like, you can save it to a "favorites" list so you don't forget it.

Scenarios

  • "I have 30 minutes to practice drawing today, but I don't know what to draw or what colors to use. Where can I get a quick idea?"
  • "I found a prompt I really like but I have to leave now; how can I save this so I can see it when I get back to my computer later?"

Color Scheme

The site uses a high-contrast dark theme to allow artwork and colorful palettes to stand out.

Primary Background: #121212

Secondary/Surface: #1E1E1E

Accent/Links: #00E5FF

Typography

Headings: Montserrat (Sans-Serif) - Bold and modern for a tech-focused creative tool.

Body Text: Open Sans (Sans-Serif) - Clean and highly legible for instructions and prompts.
